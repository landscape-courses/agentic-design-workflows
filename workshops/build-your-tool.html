WEEKS 08–14
Build Your Own Tool
The rest of the semester is yours: one tool, built by your agents, managed by you
LengthSeven classes
FormatDesk crits
OutcomeA tool you use, tagged every week
  • Run a software project: decide what comes next, every week
  • Manage agents the way a project manager manages a team
AssignmentEach week, one thing done on your tool, tagged week-NN with a message that says what you did.
WORKSHOPHow the weeks run
From Week 8 you build one tool for your own practice, in its own repository with its own CLAUDE.md. You are the developer, or better, the project manager: you decide what the tool needs next, and your agents do the work, using every way of working from Part I. Class time is desk crits. Come with what you did and what is stuck.
WORKSHOPEach week
Do one thing: a feature, a fix, a test on real studio work, a README, a peer trying your tool. Then ask Claude to tag it: Tag this commit week-09, with a message that says in one sentence what I did this week. The message is your report, for example week-09: the script now reads a whole folder of .3dm files. A tag with a clear message makes the week complete.
WORKSHOPWeek 14: Final Review
A ten-minute presentation to invited critics: the workflow before, your tool, a live demonstration, evidence of use on real work, and what was gained, lost and handed over. Your site carries a case-study page for the tool. Graded on the criteria in the syllabus.

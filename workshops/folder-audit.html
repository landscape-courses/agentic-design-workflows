WEEK 07
Aligning with Claude
Workshop: one interview, from purpose to structure to design · Discussion: Part II ideas
LengthOne class
ToolClaude desktop
BringYour site folder, a Part II idea
OutcomeSite aligned, tagged week-07
Learning Objectives
  • The agent knows only what the folder says
  • Aligning from purpose down to detail
  • One home for every fact
AssignmentYour site, aligned so a fresh session describes it the way you do, with a Part II project page holding your proposal, tagged week-07.
OngoingThe site is yours to keep after this. Align again whenever a new session gets something wrong twice.
Every session starts blank and learns your project from the folder. Six weeks of sessions have each added a line: a rule in CLAUDE.md, a decision in the design system, a task half done. Some were written twice and drifted apart. Some you stopped meaning. Claude still follows all of them.
Aligning brings the folder back to what you mean now. Claude reads everything, then interviews you, from the big picture down to the details, and rewrites the files from your answers. You approve every change.
WORKSHOPAligning with Claude
01 Align
Open your username.github.io folder in the Code tab of Claude desktop, start a fresh session, and paste:
prompt to Claude 
Let's align this folder with what I mean now, so any fresh session
works on this site the way I would.

First read everything a session reads: CLAUDE.md, the design system,
any skills, tasks/, and the site's pages. Tell me in five lines what
you think this project is and how it works.

Then interview me, one question at a time, in rounds. Start with
these, and let me add, skip, reorder or repeat any:
1. Purpose: who the site is for, what a visitor should come away
   with, what it leaves out.
2. Structure: what each file and folder holds, where a new page
   goes, how tasks/ is used.
3. Design: type, grid, color and images, one decision at a time.
4. Way of working: how I like to work with you, what you may do on
   your own, what needs my approval, when to commit and push.
Ask from what you found, starting where the files contradict each
other or the site. Ask every question as multiple choice, with the
question tool if you have one: two to four options drawn from my
files, your recommendation first and marked, and room for my own
answer. After each round, ask whether to go deeper, add a round, or
move on, also as options.

After each round, propose edits from my answers:
- Give each fact one home. Where a rule is stated twice, keep one
  and point to it.
- Cut what you can learn by reading the files.
- Phrase every rule as what to do.
- Move anything a session needs only sometimes out of CLAUDE.md into
  its own file, and point to it.
List each change with its file and why. Apply nothing until I
approve.

When the rounds are done, sort tasks/: move finished ones to
tasks/done/ and ask me before deleting any.
  • Answer in your own words, briefly. A short answer you mean beats a long one you do not.
  • Before you approve a cut, ask: would a new session still do this right?
02 Check
Close the session, open a new one, and ask what the project is. The answer should match what you said in the interview. Where it does not, fix the file.
DISCUSSIONPart II Ideas
Part II is a tool for your own design work. Bring one idea from a studio project and talk it through with the room:
  • Which step of your workflow do you repeat, dread, or do badly?
  • Which track does it fit?
  • What would the smallest useful version do on one real input?
  • How would you know it worked?
Take notes on what the room asks you. They are the first draft of your proposal.
03 Add the Project Page
Part I built the site. Part II lives on it. Back in Claude:
prompt to Claude 
Add a task and build projects/part-two.html, linked from the home
page, holding my Part II proposal: the problem, my current workflow,
the track, and what success looks like. Ask me for each part and
use my words.
  • Rename part-two.html to your project’s name once it has one. Names are context.
04 Hand It In
prompt to Claude 
Commit everything, push, then tag the latest commit week-07 and
push the tag.
Week 7 is the whole midterm: the aligned site, the project page with your proposal, and your AI Fluency certificate. Bring the link to the pin-up.

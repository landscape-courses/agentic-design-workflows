# Course: Agentic Design Workflows

Pre-semester prep for Spring 2027. Sections as in `CLAUDE.md`, "How prep works".

## Open
- Meeting day, time and room, and the first-class date: pending the Spring 2027 calendar. Once set,
  they go into `index.html` (Attendance, Weekly Schedule dates) and the first-class date into `CLAUDE.md`.
- The grade scale is LAAR 61400's graduate scale, without D grades, while 4th and 5th-year BArch
  students may enroll. Kept as LAAR 61400 has it; raise with the department if it matters.
- The course has no card on `zihao-site/teaching/index.html`; add one when it is confirmed in the catalog.

## Decided
- Part I, one way of working with an agent per week: First Session · Context through Folders ·
  Checking the Agent's Work · Standards the Agent Follows · Managing Agents · Skills · Aligning with Claude.
- Part II (Weeks 8–13) is light by design: a live log, desk crits and the student's own tool. Each
  week the student does one thing and has Claude tag it with a message saying what was done.
  Week 8 opens with two live demos (an agent-written Grasshopper Python plugin, and MCP). Week 14
  is the graded final.
- A coding-agent subscription is a department requirement, stated flatly in Course Logistics.
- Week 1 to 2 and Week 7 pages are built; Weeks 3 to 6 are stubs with their content in `week-03.md`
  to `week-06.md`.
